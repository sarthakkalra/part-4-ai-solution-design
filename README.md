# AI Solution Design for Telecom Customer Churn Prediction

## Objective
Design an AI-powered solution to predict customer churn in the telecom industry.

## Business Problem
Telecom companies lose revenue due to customer churn. The objective is to proactively identify high-risk customers.

## Proposed AI Solution
A feed-forward neural network model trained on customer behavior and subscription data.
